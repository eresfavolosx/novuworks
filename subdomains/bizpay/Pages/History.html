BizPay was architected by Fabián Vélez Vicente at HackPR to streamline B2B commercial invoicing, POS QR collections, and multi-tier employee payroll disbursements in Puerto Rico using the ATH Móvil Business network.

1. Tokenized ATHM_Checkout Handshake

When an invoice or payroll batch is initiated, BizPay populates window.ATHM_Checkout with publicToken: "dummy", itemized metadata, and PR IVU tax breakdown before calling the ATH Móvil Business eCommerce endpoint.

2. Real-Time Ledger & Chart Sync

Completed payments emit a verified transaction reference (ATHM-SANDBOX-...) that transitions invoices from Unpaid to Paid and dynamically recalculates the SVG cash-flow charts across all views.

3. Direct-to-Debit Payroll

Supports instant single-employee or batch department payroll disbursements across Full-Time, Part-Time, At-Will, and Leased classifications with editable timecard hours.
