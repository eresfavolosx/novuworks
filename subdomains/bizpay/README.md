# BizPay (`bizpay.novu.works`) — Playful Peer-to-Peer Community Micro-Economy

> *"What if splitting a dinner bill, tipping a neighborhood musician, or pooling funds for a community project felt as warm and effortless as sending a smile?"*

**Live Interactive Prototype:** [https://bizpay.novu.works](https://bizpay.novu.works) · [Explore on Novu Works](https://novu.works/#bizpay)  
**Author:** **Fabián Vélez Vicente** (`@eresfavolosx`)

---

## 🤝 The Concept
Everyday financial exchanges between friends, neighbors, and local creators often feel cold, transactional, and full of friction. **BizPay** reimagines community value exchange as a transparent, optimistic, and celebratory social interaction—where supporting a local maker or settling up after a group meal happens in a single tap.

## ⚙️ The Technology
- **Reactive Cross-Platform Architecture:** Built with **Angular**, **Ionic Capacitor**, and **TypeScript** for native-grade 60 FPS gesture physics in the browser and on mobile devices.
- **Real-Time Cloud Firestore Synchronization:** Uses **Firebase Cloud Firestore** reactive streams and atomic batch writes so both participants see celebratory confirmation states simultaneously.
- **Dynamic QR & Deep-Link Synthesis:** Encodes instant peer-to-peer payment intents into scannable visual tokens with offline-first state persistence.

## 🌱 The Impact
- Brings human warmth and instant visual clarity to everyday community exchanges.
- Removes the awkwardness of shared expenses and local micro-patronage through playful, confidence-inspiring interaction design.
