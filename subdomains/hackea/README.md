# Hackea Por Una Causa (`hackea.novu.works`) — Civic Co-Creation & Disaster-Resilient Story Hub

> *"How can we gather engineers, artists, and neighbors into a shared digital commons to build life-saving civic prototypes together?"*

**Live Interactive Prototype:** [https://hackea.novu.works](https://hackea.novu.works) · [Explore on Novu Works](https://novu.works/#hackeaporunacausa)  
**Author:** **Fabián Vélez Vicente** (`@eresfavolosx`)

---

## 🌍 The Concept
Born in Puerto Rico after hurricanes disrupted island-wide power and communication networks, **Hackea Por Una Causa** ("Hack for a Cause") was created out of optimism and collective resilience. It serves as an inclusive, bilingual digital gathering space that invites students, designers, engineers, and community leaders to turn urgent local challenges into working open-source prototypes.

## ⚙️ The Technology
- **Disaster-Resilient Web Architecture:** Hand-crafted with semantic **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and zero-dependency **JavaScript** so the entire experience loads in milliseconds even over degraded cellular or satellite connections.
- **Interactive Challenge & Team Synthesis:** Features dynamic problem-statement exploration, accessible bilingual storytelling, and streamlined team formation flows hosted on **Firebase Edge Hosting**.

## 🌱 The Impact
- Mobilized hundreds of student makers, mentors, and grass-roots organizers across Puerto Rico to co-create open-source tools for emergency relief, public education, and neighborhood resilience.
- Proved that when technology is framed around empathy and shared purpose, a local community can build extraordinary solutions under the toughest constraints.
